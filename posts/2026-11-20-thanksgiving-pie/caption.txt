The pie pop-by: one afternoon, 20 happy clients.

1. Pick your top 20 past clients
2. Text on Monday to ask who'll be home
3. Drop off a small pie Tuesday or Wednesday

"Hi {name}! I'm dropping off a little Thanksgiving pie to a few favorite clients. Will you be home Tuesday afternoon?"

Asking first means no pies on empty porches.
