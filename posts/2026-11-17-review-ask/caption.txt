Most agents ask for a review too late.

The best moment is within 48 hours of closing, while your client is excited and grateful.

"Hi {name}, would you mind leaving me a quick review? It really helps a small business like mine: {link}. Thank you!"

Send the direct link, not "find me on Google." Missed the window? Ask on their 1-year home anniversary.
