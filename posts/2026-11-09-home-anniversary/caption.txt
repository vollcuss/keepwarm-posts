The most underrated text in real estate: the home anniversary.

It's the one day a year your client is guaranteed to think about their house, and almost no agent reaches out.

Copy this:
"Happy home anniversary, {name}! {X} years ago today you got the keys to {address}. Hope it's been treating you well."

No pitch. No "just checking in." Just a real reason to say hi.

Save this post so you remember on the 1st of next month.
